# QRAIVY — Mission

Every business deserves a way to be found, remembered, and returned to —
without needing to hire a developer, a designer, or a marketing team to
make that happen.

QRAIVY exists to compress that entire chain — presence, engagement, loyalty,
and outreach — into a single QR code and the AI-assisted page behind it. A
business owner should be able to generate that presence in minutes, not
weeks, and grow it as their business grows: from a single storefront to
many locations, and from doing it themselves to handing it to an agency
that manages it on their behalf.

AI exists in this product to remove setup work, not to replace the business
owner's judgment. Every AI-generated page, message, or design is a starting
point the owner can see, edit, and make their own — never a black box, and
never the final word.

This mission doesn't change as the product grows. What changes is how much
of it is built — see `company/02_PRODUCT_VISION.md` for where things stand
today, and `company/04_DECISIONS.md` for how the major decisions along the
way have been made.
