# QRAIVY — Product Vision

## What QRAIVY Is

QRAIVY is an AI-powered customer engagement platform. At its center is a
single idea: one QR code should be able to carry a business's entire
customer relationship — a page that represents them, a way to bring
customers back, and a way to reach those customers again — generated with
AI assistance rather than built by hand.

## The Problem QRAIVY Solves

A small or mid-sized business wants a digital presence and a way to bring
customers back, without juggling a website builder, a loyalty app, a wallet
pass service, an email tool, and a push provider as five separate
subscriptions. QRAIVY's premise is that one QR code and one generated page
can be the entry point to all of it, with AI doing the setup work a
business owner would otherwise have to do by hand — writing copy, picking a
layout, drafting a campaign message.

## Who QRAIVY Is For

QRAIVY starts with **local businesses** — a single storefront, restaurant,
gym, or service business that wants a presence today, quickly. From there,
the platform is intended to grow to serve **multi-location brands and
franchises**, where one business identity spans many physical locations,
and **agencies** who manage QRAIVY on behalf of many client businesses at
once, under their own brand.

The single-business experience is the foundation everything else is built
on top of — not a simplified version of a more "complete" enterprise
product, but the core loop the rest of the vision extends outward from.

## Core Product Pillars

**AI Business Generation** — the preferred way for a business to get
started: point QRAIVY at your existing website, and AI generates a first
draft of your page, its copy, and its structure. This is the front door to
the product, not an optional add-on.

**Smart Landing Pages** — the object everything else attaches to. Every QR
code leads to one; every wallet pass, loyalty program, and push campaign is
scoped to one.

**Wallet Passes** — Apple and Google Wallet integration, so a customer's
loyalty progress lives on their phone's lock screen, not in a separate app
they have to remember to open.

**Loyalty** — stamp-based progress toward a reward, tracked per business and
per customer, redeemable in person via QR scan or NFC tap.

**Deals** — time-bound offers a business can create and promote through the
same page and channels as everything else, distinct from ongoing loyalty
progress.

**Push Campaigns** — a single action that reaches a customer everywhere
they've opted in: their wallet pass, their browser, and their inbox.

**Multi-location** — a brand with many physical locations should be able to
manage them under one identity, with loyalty, analytics, and campaigns that
can operate per-location or roll up to the brand as a whole.

**Agency / White-label** — agencies should be able to manage many client
businesses under their own brand, not QRAIVY's, with the same tools they'd
use for a single client scaled to many.

The first three pillars are where QRAIVY's core product experience lives
today. The remaining pillars — Deals, Multi-location, and Agency/White-label
— represent where the platform is headed as it grows beyond a single
business's needs. Building toward them should never come at the cost of the
core loop that already works.

## Product Philosophy

One QR code, one page, one growing set of engagement tools — the product's
coherence comes from everything hanging off that single entry point, not
from a suite of disconnected tools that happen to share a login. New
capability should be judged by how much setup work it removes for the
business owner, not just by whether it's technically useful. And where two
mechanisms could solve the same problem, QRAIVY should converge on one that
works well rather than maintain two that half-work.

## AI Philosophy

AI generates a starting point a human can edit — never a replacement for
the business owner's judgment, and never a black box. AI-assisted content —
page copy, campaign messages, generated designs — should always be visibly
attributable and editable. As AI capability expands across the product, it
should converge toward one consistent, well-built implementation rather
than accumulate as separate one-off integrations.

## Customer Experience Philosophy

The person scanning the QR code — the end customer — should need no
account, no app install, and no login for the core experience: scan, view
the page, optionally add a wallet pass, optionally opt in to hear from the
business again. Consent, once given, should be treated as a real
commitment — enforced, not just collected as a formality. And whatever a
customer sees reflected back to them — their stamp count, their reward
status — should always be their real, current state, never stale or wrong.

## Long-Term Vision

QRAIVY grows from single-location businesses toward multi-location brands,
franchises, and agencies managing many client businesses under one account
— while keeping the core "one QR, one page, one growing set of tools" loop
intact for the simplest use case. The long-term shape of the enterprise
model is Brand → Locations → Landing Pages: one brand identity, many
physical locations under it, and each location's presence still expressed
through the same Smart Landing Page mechanism the whole platform is built
on. Growth toward this model should be additive to what exists today, not a
replacement for it.

## What QRAIVY Is Not

QRAIVY is not a general-purpose website builder — pages are built around
specific business use cases, not designed freeform from a blank canvas for
arbitrary purposes. It is not a system of record for a business's core
operations — point of sale, inventory, scheduling — but an engagement layer
that sits on top of a business that already exists. And it is not a
generic digital business-card product; wherever that idea has appeared in
the codebase, it has not been the platform's direction.
