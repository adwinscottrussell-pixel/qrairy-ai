Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a Smart QR page?

Log in to your dashboard and click "+ Create New Smart QR". Follow the 5-step wizard to set up your AI-powered landing page in under 60 seconds.

How do I upgrade my plan?

Go to your dashboard → Billing & Plans → choose the plan that fits your needs. Upgrades take effect immediately.

Can I customise my landing page?

Yes — after publishing, click "Manage" on any page from your dashboard to edit colours, sections, voice messages and more.

How do wallet passes work?

Your customers can add your Smart QR loyalty card to their Apple or Google Wallet directly from your landing page. They collect stamps automatically on each visit.

What languages are supported?

Qraivy automatically detects the language of your website and generates your landing page and voice welcome in that language. Currently English and German are fully supported.
