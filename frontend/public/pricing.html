Frequently asked questions

What is the AI landing page?
When a customer scans your QR code they land on a branded page with an AI assistant that knows about your business. It can answer questions, show your services and encourage customers to subscribe for special offers.
How do push notifications work?
Customers subscribe when they visit your AI landing page. You can then send AI-generated special offers directly to their device from your dashboard — no app required.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time. You will keep access until the end of your billing period.
Do you offer EUR pricing?
Yes — toggle to EUR above. We also offer a German language version of the platform. Coming soon.
What happens to my QR codes if I downgrade?
Your QR codes will continue to redirect but the AI landing page and push notification features will be disabled until you upgrade again.
